About

Bay Area singer-songwriter with Folk and Americana Roots

Bay Area singer-songwriter Gian Achen writes nostalgic Americana folk songs that blend heartfelt storytelling with resonant melodies. Drawing comparisons to artists like Elliott Smith and Justin Townes Earle, Gian’s music is honest, understated, and a refreshing callback to the golden era of ’60s and ’70s songwriters. Through his intimate live performances, Gian creates a musical experience that is both familiar and uniquely his own.
